Use Analytics to see how an account and its posts performed over time. It includes posts you published through SocialFlow.ai and, where the provider allows it, posts published elsewhere. Those external posts appear in reports, not in **Publications**, which holds work you authored in SocialFlow.ai.

## Turn on collection for an account

1. Open **Settings → Workspace → Social accounts** and select an account.
2. Enable **Analytics** if the account supports it. If the option is unavailable, check the permissions and explanation shown beside it.
3. Open **Analytics** and allow the first collection to finish. Check the last successful update before using the figures.

Disabling Analytics stops future collection for that account. It keeps the history already saved and does not disconnect the account.

## Compare the right posts

Choose an account and date range. Use **All content**, **Published with SocialFlow.ai**, or **Published elsewhere** to choose which posts to compare. Follower counts describe the whole account. Views and engagement describe individual posts.

Read each measurement's label. Views, impressions, and reach mean different things, and providers do not all return them. A blank measurement is not zero. If the page shows a coverage notice, the account may be newly connected, lack permission, or have only part of its provider history available.

Open a content row or insight to see the posts behind it. Compare similar formats over similar periods before deciding that a topic or time worked better.

## Export your analytics

Choose **Export** to download the data behind the page for the selected date range. The account filter starts with the account selected on the page.

- **Format.** CSV opens in any spreadsheet or script. Excel includes every table plus charts. JSON is for tools.
- **Timezone.** Publish times and day, week, and month groups use the timezone you choose. Daily trend rows always use UTC calendar days, like the charts.
- **Group rows by.** Keep one row per post, or total the results by day, week, month, platform, or account.
- **Table.** For CSV and JSON, choose the grouped report, posts, daily trends, or accounts. Excel always contains all of them.
- **Accounts.** Select accounts by their avatar, platform, and handle, including multiple accounts on the same platform. Choose **All accounts** to remove the account filter. Your selection applies to every table.
- **Post metric columns.** Choose the metric columns for the report and posts tables. Leave the selection empty to include every measured metric across the full range. Daily trends and accounts keep their own columns.

Totals grouped by date, platform, or account sum lifetime counters. Provider averages and reporting-period measurements remain available in the posts table, but stay blank in grouped reports because they cannot be summed by publication date. Per-post values retain their stored integer units, including milliseconds for durations and basis points for percentages.

A blank cell means the measurement is missing or cannot be aggregated for that report. It is not zero. Scripts with `api:read` access can use `GET /analytics/export`. Pass comma-separated `account_ids` to select accounts, and `platforms` to limit platforms. See the [API reference](/api-reference).

## Update or investigate missing data

SocialFlow.ai collects results in the background, more often for new posts than old ones. **Refresh data** requests another collection. It may still wait for a provider limit or retry delay.

If data is missing:

- Check that Analytics is enabled for the selected account.
- Inspect its connection status and reconnect if a required permission expired.
- Check the last update and coverage notice before comparing totals.
- Wait until a temporary provider limit expires, then retry the refresh.

A provider may revise its numbers after publication. The latest saved result can therefore differ from an earlier report or the provider's live interface.

## Make another post from a result

Choose **Repurpose** on a content item. SocialFlow.ai opens a new, unsaved composer and asks you to review the direction before requesting AI-generated copy. Check the idea, destination accounts, and final text before saving or publishing.

To check whether a post arrived, open it in **Publications** and inspect each destination. To investigate a delivery error, open the post and use the [delivery troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting).
